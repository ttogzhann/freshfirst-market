## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01 - Complete older-history access:** Given at least seven distinct persisted cooking records with known timestamps, recipe titles and recorded quantities, initial Dashboard shows its existing newest-five recent list. An explicit, discoverable control opens the complete history (expansion, dialog or equivalent simple view). Every record, including sixth/seventh oldest entries, is reachable newest-first by `cooked_at`, with no accidental five-record truncation, duplicate or omission. Equal timestamps need no invented chronological tie-breaker.
- **AC-02 - Faithful record presentation:** Each displayed row includes the stored recipe title, readable cooking date/time and exact recorded recipe quantity. Preserve existing `recipeQuantity(record.servings)` semantics where history lacks `base_servings`; do not infer a source batch multiplier or human servings from absent metadata. For genuine baseline recipes with verified `base_servings=1`, recorded values 1 and 8 correspond to one and eight whole-source batches. Keep neutral recipe-quantity wording for older/general records rather than rewriting or reinterpreting their values. History remains read-only and no API/schema extension is required.
- **AC-03 - Loading, empty, failure and retry:** With a delayed initial `listHistory` response, show a visible loading state; with an empty response, show a clear empty-history state; with a controlled failure, show an actionable error and retry. Retry after recovery loads all returned records without fabricating rows or making any mutation. Loading/error must not be presented as an empty successful result. Existing Dashboard content remains available and returning/collapsing the browser is possible.
- **AC-04 - Reload and restart durability:** Given the seven real persisted records, reload the frontend and reopen complete history, then restart the actual backend process against the same disposable file-backed SQLite database and reopen again. All original record IDs, stored titles, timestamps and recorded quantities remain available in newest-first order. Browsing/reloading does not create, modify or delete history, pantry, basket or shopping state. A discovered storage/API-loss defect remains core integrity work; report/escalate it rather than hiding it as an enhancement limitation.
- **AC-05 - Existing API, default UI and usability:** Complete history reuses existing `useHistory` / `FreshService.listHistory` / GET `/api/cooking/history`; preserve all 23 operations and the default Dashboard newest-five list, recommendations and stats. On fresh load, older rows are not automatically expanded. No sidebar destination/redesign is introduced. At 1280x800 and 375x812, a user can open, browse through the final older row and return/collapse with keyboard, visible focus and readable unclipped row content; no horizontal page scrolling prevents access.
